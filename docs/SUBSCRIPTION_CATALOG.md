## Money Talk private-beta products

Project Commander, Job Hunter Lite, Move OS, Subscription Killer, Scam Detector, Business Brain, Model Router, Model Olympics, Creator Engine, Agent Factory, Life OS and Trading Lab are local-first prototypes.

They are not marketed as autonomous AI products yet. The initial beta is software access/customization/support; hosted accounts, sync, model calls and production automation come only after user demand and appropriate permissions.
