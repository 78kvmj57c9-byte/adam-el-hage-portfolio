## Safety / truth boundaries

- No product claims live integrations unless they are actually connected.
- No production subscription infrastructure is bought before a buyer need is proven.
- No live trading in Trading Lab; paper evidence only.
- Scam Detector reports heuristic red flags, not proof of fraud.
- Human approval remains required before payments, public auto-posting, live broker activity, sensitive data collection or other consequential automation.
- Final Boss remains in development and is not marketed as a finished professional-level autonomous product.
