TACTICS LAB.

Stop copying TikTok tactics without knowing what problem they solve. This room teaches shape, spacing, overloads, rest defence, build-up routes, pressing triggers and the trade-offs every setup creates.

Interactive pitch prototype

BUILD-UP.

Stretch the first line, create a free player and keep enough structure behind the ball to survive the turnover.

Being pressed to death?Start from the free player and the next passing lane, not a random formation code.
Can't create chances?Look at occupation, width, half-spaces and who actually threatens the last line.
Conceding every counter?Rest defence is the shape you keep while attacking, not what you remember after losing the ball.
