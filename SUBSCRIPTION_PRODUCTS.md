# Subscription Product Catalogue

These are working prototype products designed to start as a managed monthly service. Prices below are test offers, not guaranteed market rates.

## OpsPulse — monthly operations reporting
- **Starter:** €79/month — one monthly CSV/report refresh, 5 KPIs, one dashboard, one correction round.
- **Growth:** €149/month — up to 3 source files, 10 KPIs, monthly quality checks and summary notes.
- **Ops:** €299/month — up to 5 sources, 15 KPIs, monthly report, anomaly list and one 30-minute review.
- Client-owned source files; no accounting or tax interpretation.

## ReviewPulse — customer feedback intelligence
- **Starter:** €69/month — one feedback CSV, rating summary, low-score list and top recurring terms.
- **Growth:** €129/month — up to 3 channels/exports, trend comparison and prioritized issue list.
- **Pro:** €249/month — recurring monthly analysis, custom categories and management summary.
- No fake-review generation and no scraping without permission.

## LeadPulse — simple lead follow-up dashboard
- **Starter:** €59/month — up to 100 active leads and one monthly cleanup/report.
- **Growth:** €119/month — up to 500 leads, pipeline summary and follow-up queue.
- **Pro:** €229/month — custom fields, monthly pipeline review and one small improvement request.
- Integrations are quoted separately after API/access review.

## StockPulse — inventory/reorder monitor
- **Starter:** €59/month — up to 100 SKUs and one monthly stock report.
- **Growth:** €119/month — up to 500 SKUs, reorder alerts and valuation summary.
- **Pro:** €229/month — custom categories, recurring import cleanup and monthly review.
- Does not replace an ERP; destructive inventory writes are excluded unless separately scoped and tested.

## MenuMargin — restaurant menu profitability
- **Starter:** €69/month — up to 40 menu items, food-cost input and margin dashboard.
- **Growth:** €129/month — up to 100 items, category comparison and low-margin review.
- **Pro:** €249/month — monthly refresh, custom targets and one management review call.
- Tax/accounting advice and POS integrations are separate.

## QuoteFlow — quote and margin calculator
- **Setup:** €149 one-time for a branded template based on an agreed schema.
- **Care:** €49/month for one small change/update and defect support.
- **Business:** €99/month for up to 3 small updates, new service rows and monthly review.

## Commercial rules
1. Start with a paid pilot or setup fee; do not buy infrastructure before a customer exists.
2. Confirm inputs, users, output, acceptance checks, privacy constraints and response window before promising delivery.
3. Client pays for any required hosting/API/subscription costs unless the written quote says otherwise.
4. Monthly plans are capped. New integrations, redesigns, migrations and urgent work are separately quoted.
5. A subscription is sold only after the prototype or pilot actually solves the buyer's recurring problem.
